## 🤖 AI-powered timetable generation

Say goodbye to entering every lecture manually.

1. Upload an image of your college timetable.
2. Let the Google Gemini API extract relevant schedule information.
3. Set up your weekly class schedule using the extracted details.

Spend less time entering data and more time preparing for class.

## 📊 Attendance you can actually plan around

Know where you stand before deciding to skip a lecture.

- Track attendance separately for every subject.
- Monitor your attendance percentage.
- Review your attendance history through daily logs.
- Estimate how many upcoming classes you can miss while maintaining your target attendance percentage.
- Record classes as attended, missed, or cancelled.

Whether your college requires 75% attendance or another threshold, MyCollegeMate helps you plan around your target.

> **Note:** Attendance calculations depend on the records and target percentage configured in the app. Always follow your college's official attendance rules.

## 🔒 Privacy & data security

MyCollegeMate is designed to help you manage academic information while making the app's data practices clear.

### 💾 Academic data and local storage

Academic information, including subjects, timetables, attendance records, and notes, is designed to be stored locally on your device.

**Important:** Data stored only on your device may be permanently lost if you uninstall the app or clear its app data. Keep backups of important notes and documents.

### 🤖 Google Gemini AI

When you use AI timetable scanning, the selected timetable image or relevant information may be sent to Google's services for processing.

Avoid uploading images containing sensitive information that is not needed to generate your schedule.

### 📈 Analytics and crash reporting

MyCollegeMate uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to help understand app usage, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability.

Depending on the implementation and device settings, these services may process app interaction events, diagnostic information, device information, and crash reports.

### 📺 Advertising and payments

Advertising and billing providers may process information needed to display ads, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and process purchases. The information involved depends on the provider, SDK configuration, and applicable privacy settings.

For complete details about data collection, processing, and your rights, refer to the app's published Privacy Policy.
