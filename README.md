## 📖 About the App

Welcome to **MyCollegeMate**, your personal academic companion for Android. From tracking attendance and managing class schedules to organizing study materials, MyCollegeMate helps you stay on top of your college life.

With AI-powered timetable scanning, smart reminders, and convenient attendance tracking, managing your academic routine becomes simpler and more efficient.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Timetable Scanner

Generate your class schedule without manually entering every lecture. Upload an image of your timetable and let the Google Gemini API extract the relevant schedule information to help set up your weekly classes.

### 📱 Home Screen Widgets & Notifications

Stay informed about your academic schedule at a glance. Access relevant class information through supported home screen widgets and notifications without constantly opening the app.

### 📊 Smart Attendance Tracking

Track attendance for individual subjects, monitor your attendance percentage, and understand how many classes you can miss while maintaining your target attendance percentage.

### 📅 Daily Logs & Interactive Schedule

Review your daily timetable and record classes as attended, missed, or cancelled to keep your attendance history organized.

### 🗂️ Notes & Document Organization

Import and organize notes, documents, and PDFs by subject. Share documents directly from supported third-party applications into your MyCollegeMate folders.

### ⏰ Smart Reminders

Receive timely notifications for upcoming lectures and your academic routine, helping you stay organized throughout the semester.

### 💎 Premium Features & In-App Purchases

Unlock premium app features through available in-app purchases and subscription options. Choose the plan that best suits your academic needs.

### 📺 Rewarded Ads

Free users can watch rewarded advertisements to unlock eligible app features. This provides an alternative way to access supported premium functionality without purchasing a subscription.

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Security

Your privacy matters. MyCollegeMate is designed to help you manage your academic life while limiting unnecessary access to personal information.

### 💾 Personal App Data

Academic information such as subjects, timetables, attendance records, and notes is designed to be stored locally on your device.

**Important data notice:** If your academic data is stored only locally, uninstalling the app or clearing its data may permanently remove saved information. Keep your own backups of important documents.

### 🤖 AI Timetable Scanner

The timetable scanning feature uses the Google Gemini API. When you scan a timetable, the selected image or relevant information may be transmitted to Google's services for processing and extraction of schedule details.

Avoid uploading images containing sensitive personal information that is not needed for timetable scanning.

### 📊 Analytics & Crash Reporting

MyCollegeMate uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to help understand app usage, identify crashes, and improve reliability.

Depending on the SDK configuration and device settings, these services may collect diagnostic information, app interaction events, device information, and crash reports.

### 📺 Advertising & Billing

The app displays rewarded advertisements and offers in-app purchases. Advertising and billing providers may process information as necessary to serve ads, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and complete transactions.

The information processed depends on the providers used, SDK configuration, and applicable privacy settings. Please refer to the app's published Privacy Policy for further details.
